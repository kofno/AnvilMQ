Evaluation release. Broadens read-only introspection, enqueue-time safety controls, and tenant fairness, and adds a self-contained local example and a benchmark harness. Single broker; no HA, authentication, or TLS.

- Read-only failures feed (`/v1/failures`) backed by a WAL reader, with a packaged Grafana failures dashboard.
- Read-only job and history search (`/v1/search`), with opt-in FTS5 full-text and prefix-wildcard matching, plus a read-only single-job detail endpoint (`GET /v1/jobs/{id}`).
- Built-in read-only search console at `/console`, including click-through to single-job detail.
- Built-in parentage: the server derives execution depth and offers client `enqueueChild`, guarded by an always-on execution-depth circuit breaker (maximum depth 10).
- Ancestry validation with opt-in runaway-chain quarantine (bounds total lineage size; disabled by default).
- Sliding-window ingress velocity controls (admission-side rate limiting keyed on facet).
- Durable enqueue-rejection records with a read-only `/v1/rejections` feed, kind-labeled `anvilmq_enqueue_rejections_total`, and retention-based pruning.
- Opt-in equal round-robin tenant fairness on the claim path.
- Opt-in all-names per-name metrics mode and per-function dashboards (importable JSON with optional Helm sidecar packaging).
- Background retention sweeper that prunes job history, receipts, and rejection records in one pass.

Use the matching `anvilmq-client-0.1.0-rc.3.tgz` client and `anvilmq-0.1.0-rc.3.tgz` Helm chart, with the observability bundle in `anvilmq-observability-0.1.0-rc.3.tgz`. The broker image targets Linux amd64. Check `SHA256SUMS` and pin the image digest from `image.txt`.

Startup adds new tables and indexes (full-text search and durable enqueue-rejection records). Allow time and storage for index creation on an existing backlog, and take a storage-consistent backup before upgrading. Helm rollback does not revert database contents. Retained history, receipts, and rejection records are pruned by the background retention sweeper on configurable age/count bounds; monitor PVC usage.

This remains a single-broker evaluation deployment without HA, authentication, or TLS. Use an isolated namespace/PVC and redirected or disabled business side effects. Worker processing remains at least once; handler side effects still need idempotency. The client is intended for Bun and is not a drop-in replacement for the incumbent queue client.

A self-contained local example is available under `examples/sim/`, and an isolated latency/throughput benchmark harness under `harness/` documents methodology and local, exploratory results (not SLOs).

See [deployment instructions](https://github.com/kofno/AnvilMQ/blob/v0.1.0-rc.3/docs/release-and-deploy.md) and [observability setup](https://github.com/kofno/AnvilMQ/blob/v0.1.0-rc.3/docs/observability.md).
